## Attempts to break the mathematical steps

* **Binary reduction and boundaries.** A threshold at the larger of two unequal values distinguishes them even with ties. On an adjacent cover the selected bit exhausts the entire mass difference. A zero/one exchange on the lower layer has a common upper neighbour; its two lower images must coincide. Weight zero, weight n, and minimum/maximum selection are covered. A one-element value domain alone would not specify this optimization problem.
* **First reuse.** Consider an empty network, a matching, an immediately repeated pair, and a gate joining two earlier pairs. A matching leaves all supports of size at most two. At first reuse the support has size at most four even when the two parents share an earlier gate. Choosing the sign from the reused output forces the same extreme through both gates. A balanced layer supplies two different ordinary assignments forcing that wire to vary; retention of this variation depends on the absence of a future gate, not on coexistence with a sentinel assignment.
* **Deletion.** The test used both extremal signs, arbitrary orientations, repeated pairs, tied ordinary data and every sentinel input. The mathematical invariant matches the entire visible tuple at each gate, so fixed routing is accounted for. At an endpoint rank the deleted sentinel may itself be the answer; only the trivial encounter bound applies in that case.
* **Recursive reduction.** At width eleven the boundary weights are 5,6. At width ten the possible ranks 5,6 give weights 5,6 and 4,5 respectively. Weight five works at each stage. The final alternatives are (9,4), (9,5), (9,6). Rank duality is applied only in the oriented model. The lower bound is 19+3+3; an exhaustive 11-wire witness independently supplies the upper bound.
* **Search completeness.** A solution can stop at any prefix; every visited node tests the empty suffix. Reversing an orientation conjugates every later gate and the output. Deleting a gate that is one fixed permutation on the **union** of the images preserves separation and shortens a suffix. A shortest hypothetical completion must survive these reductions. Equal column sums alone, independent permutations for the layers, or references to active nodes would all lack the stated justification.
* **Dependency order.** Every root begins with both complete layers. A trace is an upper limit on how many gates a particular sentinel can encounter in a hypothetical full solution. C++ reconstructs its extent by reading the prefix backwards. Every nontrivial imported bound has width n−1 and was replayed earlier. The last two claims are a redundancy check sharing prerequisites, not additional independent base cases.

## Execution evidence

The individual commands, exit codes, timings, hashes, per-claim node counts and scope of the Lean check are recorded in [the reproducibility record](reproducibility.md). Both checkers accepted all 26 claims, with identical per-claim stdout. All 27 certificate files were regenerated exactly. A backward BFS over full Boolean functions recovered all 20 oriented rank-selection optima at widths 2–6. Its only reduction is equality of truth tables. The existing forward small-width audit was also executed.

The two upper-bound evaluators agreed on all 2,048 binary inputs. All 177,147 ternary tuples passed. All 25 single-gate removals failed. There were 407,160 full-tuple deletion checks. The independent parser rejected 25 targeted failures and 32 random illegal bytes inserted into valid streams. For 32 seeded random changes between legal opcodes both checkers agreed, including two alternative proofs they both accepted. Semantic acceptance of those alternative reasons is expected; their bytes differ from the distributed artifact.

## Originality and search scope

The search covered historical selection and median-filter papers, the current construction catalogue, full-text arXiv search, and web queries including repository and preprint domains. Terms included “median of eleven”, “11 inputs”, “25 comparators”, “median circuit”, “partition network”, “single rank selection”, “extreme path”, and “optimal compare-and-swap”. Reference chains were followed from the catalogue, Tomar and the GSI report. These searches cannot certify that no obscure, unpublished or differently indexed proof exists.

* Dobbelaere publishes the exact witness, including the ordered pairs. The eleven-input table gives (25,11) and (26,8) without a proof of size optimality. [Catalogue and witness](https://bertdobbelaere.github.io/median_networks.html#N11L25D11)
* Sekanina's 2004 Table 3 lists **26** compare-swaps at width eleven and **42** live min/max subcomponents. The 2017 filter paper calls its seeds optimal in an experimental context; its centre-weighted use of eleven wires duplicates one pixel. That passage supplies no exclusion of 24 comparators on eleven independent inputs. The 2008 adaptive-filter Table I omits width eleven. [2004 paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-24653-4_25) · [2017 paper, p. 628](https://www.radioeng.cz/fulltexts/2017/17_03_0623_0632.pdf) · [2008 Table I](https://www.vut.cz/www_base/vav_priloha.php?prilohaID=278748&vavID=27767)
* Afshani et al., Lemmas 17–18, prove that selecting a single rank fixes the partition of the output positions. Threshold the distinct ordered keys on either side of the selected rank to obtain our two-layer conclusion. The Boolean cover proof provides a short route to this known structure. [arXiv:1901.02857v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.02857v2)
* Harder's residual-set deletion and Isabelle/HOL checker concern full sorting. Tomar's single-extreme step for unordered top-t selection saves two comparisons; his sorting paper follows two extreme values. The exact three-encounter lemma under the balanced-layer hypothesis was not located in these works. Generic extreme deletion and wire relabelling have clear precedents. [Harder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.04400) · [Tomar on selection](https://www.mefyi.com/papers/selection-networks-three-four-largest/) · [Tomar on sorting](https://www.mefyi.com/papers/sorting-networks-13-inputs-45/)

Its full proofs and certificates are offered to reviewers on request. This audit examined the public report and replayed this repository's own bytes; it did not replay GSI's private artifacts. [GSI report and table](https://generalised.si/research/moore-bound-defect-two)
* Classical partition selection, SAT-aided sorting proofs, and certified clausal proofs provide related techniques with different specifications. [Yao](https://doi.org/10.1137/0209043) · [Pippenger](https://doi.org/10.1137/0220054) · [Codish et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcss.2015.11.014) · [LRAT](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-63046-5_14)

**Contribution supported by this review.** A matching eleven-input lower bound, a short reduction to two known nine-input inequalities, and a public reproducible exclusion transcript. No prior proof of the matching eleven-input bound was found. The known construction, the nine-input values, the fixed-partition implication and the general idea of deletion should receive no novelty claim here.

## Residual trust and publication decision

Lean checks the general cover and boundary implications given the map hypotheses; it does not verify the complete theorem. The remaining arguments and protocol rely on the mathematical proofs, correct execution of at least one checker, and ordinary compiler/runtime trust. A small-width BFS or mutation campaign alone could not establish the nine-input exclusions. The historical heuristic and SAT logs were inventoried and inspected. Their expensive searches were not rerun and none contribute proof evidence.

For an arXiv preprint the limited claim and reproducible artifact are appropriate. Before journal submission, have an external specialist examine the three-encounter lemma, the cache transfer and historical priority, especially the median-filter and selection literature. For an archive deposit the author should confirm authorship/AI disclosure, choose redistribution terms and cite the immutable version. No DOI or submission is asserted.

## Adversarial acceptance review for 1.0.0-rc.3

The earlier verdict above describes the first audited candidate. The new candidate passes computational replay and formalizes its proof rules. A stable formal verification release remains blocked on concrete kernel acceptance of the nine-input bounds.

| Evidence level | Acceptance |
|---|---|
| A · Kernel-checked | Network semantics, binary reduction, boundary constancy, three-comparison extreme path, moving-bijection deletion, recurrence, generic opcode soundness, all witness inputs, and concrete replays through width six. The final statement is conditional. |
| B · Independent computational checks | Python and C++ accept all 26 claims. The executable Lean checker cross-checks their verdicts. Reverse truth-table search independently recovers sizes at widths two through six. Witness, negative tests, figures and regeneration are covered by the verification script. |
| C · Mathematical arguments outside the checked claim | Applying the formalized recurrence to the principal nine-wire exclusions relies on computer-assisted evidence. The correspondence of on-disk gzip framing and the rendered input is checked by tools outside the kernel. |
| D · Historical originality | The search located no prior proof of the eleven-input result. Published precedents in structure, construction and nine-wire bounds are cited. Independent human confirmation is pending. |

- **Model audit.** Wire distinctness and index range are intrinsic to the Lean model. The size proof uses the larger oriented model. A certificate prefix is constrained by an explicit root budget and every candidate branch either represents a redundant move, exceeds a proved path cap, or is checked recursively.
- **Memo counterexample check.** Equal column sums are insufficient. Replay checks that the previous layer rows lie in the current layer images under a single permutation. This direction suffices because separating the larger set also separates its subset. A saved exclusion occurs only after all of the earlier expansion's children close.
- **Runtime limits.** The largest concrete kernel run was killed during the attempt. The executable Lean run and the Python/C++ replays cannot close the Lean obligation.
- **Attribution.** The primary Xcell scan gives John L. Smith as its byline. It describes its nine-input circuit as minimal and gives no size-exclusion proof. Dobbelaere's catalogue and its identical SorterHunter source were cross-checked against the 25 pairs. The upstream MIT notice accompanies the attributed data.
- **Release gate.** Preserve v1.0.0-rc.1 and v1.0.0-rc.2. Create the stable tag after the unconditional theorem, full regression checks and CI pass on the same stable commit. This report is internal and AI-assisted.
