{\LARGE\bfseries Research Summary for External Review\par}
{\large Optimality of a 25-Comparator Median Selection Network on Eleven Inputs\par}
Rocco Martino \hfill \texttt{1.0.0-rc.3} \hfill 20 November 2026

\textbf{Claim.} Exactly 25 comparators suffice to select the sixth smallest of eleven values on wire 5 in a fixed compare--exchange network with unrestricted depth. The upper bound is Bert Dobbelaere's published 25-comparator construction. The proposed contribution is a matching lower bound excluding every 24-comparator network, with either comparator orientation and any fixed output allowed in the exclusion argument.

\textbf{Argument.} On the two Boolean layers straddling the selection threshold, monotonicity and preservation of Hamming weight force the complete output vector to be constant on each layer. At the first reuse of a wire, an extreme input can traverse two comparisons. Its current output still varies on a sufficiently balanced boundary layer, so it must meet a third comparator. Deleting that extreme leaves a smaller network and saves at least three comparisons. Two deletions reduce the exclusion to ranks four and five on nine inputs, with rank six supplied by duality.

\textbf{Evidence.} Standalone Python and C++ checkers accepted the 174,008-node certificate for 26 exclusions. The witness passed all 2,048 binary inputs. Lean 4 checks the witness, the structural lemmas, the soundness of the replay rules and eleven concrete replays through width six. The final Lean optimality statement still takes the two nine-wire lower bounds as hypotheses. The complete executable Lean replay provides a computational cross-check. Independent human mathematical review and historical priority remain open.

\textbf{Feedback sought.} Is there an earlier proof of size optimality for eleven inputs? Please scrutinize the choice of the balanced boundary layer at the first reused wire, the moving-bijection deletion proof, and the soundness of reusing a completed exclusion under a common wire permutation when the two prefixes have different extreme traces.
